# nextlevel. — Brand Identity Guide

## About the Company

**NextLevel** (stylized as **nextlevel.**) is an independent software studio based in **Córdoba, Argentina**, founded in 2025. They design and build custom websites and web platforms for Argentine businesses — particularly small and medium-sized commercial businesses (PyMEs).

## Brand Essence

- **Industry**: Software / Web design & development
- **Type**: Independent studio (one-person operation)
- **Location**: Córdoba, Argentina
- **Founded**: 2025
- **Core Services**: Custom web design, e-commerce, SEO & performance
- **Target Audience**: Argentine business owners (dueños de comercios)
- **Domain**: nextlvl.com.ar

## Brand Personality

- **Editorial & crafted** — every detail is intentional, nothing is generic
- **Direct & honest** — no jargon, no hidden costs, real timelines
- **Small but mighty** — "Un estudio chico que piensa en grande"
- **Warm minimalism** — modern and clean but never cold or corporate
- **Hands-on** — direct client relationship, no intermediaries

## Tagline

**"software que lleva tu marca al siguiente nivel."**

## Value Proposition

> "NextLevel es un estudio de software independiente. Diseñamos y construimos sitios y plataformas web pensadas para escalar."

## Key Differentiators

1. **Direct relationship** — no intermediaries, no enormous teams
2. **Fixed pricing** — detailed proposals with scope, timelines, and fixed price
3. **Full transparency** — clients can see progress at every stage
4. **Post-launch support** — available for adjustments after go-live
5. **In-house everything** — designed and developed in-house
